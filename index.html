Toddler Guard – Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 4, 2026

Toddler Guard ("the app") is developed by Thanh Nguyen ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and your choices. We built Toddler Guard to collect as little as possible.

Summary

  • Toddler Guard itself does not collect, store on any server, sell, or share your personal information.
  • Your settings stay on your device.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some device data to show and measure ads.
  • Purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details.

Information stored on your device

The app saves a few settings locally on your phone so it works the way you set it up: your remaining free-lock time and saved locks, whether you bought "Unlock forever", your unlock code, auto-unlock timer, and lock badge preference. This information never leaves your device and is deleted if you uninstall the app.

Accessibility service

Toddler Guard uses Android's Accessibility service only to provide its core features:

  • Detect your volume-button unlock sequence, to lock and unlock the touch screen and screen rotation.
  • See which app is open when you lock, so it can return you to that app if Home or Recents is opened while locked.
  • Close the notification panel if it is pulled down while locked.
  • Notice when an incoming call alert appears, so the screen unlocks automatically and you can answer. Only the type of alert is checked (whether it is an incoming call), never its contents.

The Accessibility service does not read the content of your screen, your notifications, or anything you type, and no information it handles is recorded, stored, or sent anywhere. You can turn it off at any time in your phone's Settings → Accessibility.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

If you use the free version, the app can show rewarded video ads, but only when you choose to watch one. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device model, operating system version, and general location (country or region). This information is collected and handled by Google, not by us.

Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or limit ad personalization, in your phone's Settings → Google → All services → Ads (location may vary by device).

Buying "Unlock forever" removes all ads.

Purchases (Google Play)

The optional "Unlock forever" purchase is processed entirely by Google Play. We do not receive or store your name, payment card, or billing details. The app only checks with Google Play whether your Google account owns the purchase, so it can unlock Premium features on your devices.

Children

Toddler Guard is a tool for parents and adults to lock their own phone's screen before handing it to a child. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are shown only on the app's main screen, which is used by the parent, and never while the screen is locked.

Data sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information. Apart from the Google services described above (AdMob and Google Play), the app does not send data to any third party.

Data security and deletion

Because the app keeps its settings only on your device, you can delete all app data at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it. To delete advertising data held by Google, use your device's ad settings or Google's privacy tools linked above.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above.
